What it does

Everything your table rolls, on screen

1

Captures everyone's rolls

Auto-detects public rolls from every player — no special format to type. Private whispers and GM rolls are never shown.

2

3D dice that match the result

The physical dice land on the exact value rolled. For advantage or disadvantage, both d20s roll and the right one is shown.

3

Several rolls at once

Every roll is its own throw with its own plaque, so simultaneous rolls from different players never queue up — the plaques line up in a row that reflows as dice land and settle.

4

An overlay that actually looks good

The Arcane Plaque shows the portrait, big total, roll name, dice breakdown, advantage badge, and crit or fumble tags.

5

Design your own plaque

A WYSIWYG editor lets you pick frame art from a template library, recolor it, drag the zones around, and add a custom background. The overlay renders exactly what you build.

6

Per-player dice styles

Start from a curated preset, then fine-tune material, texture, and per-channel colors — numbers, body, edge — with a live preview.

7

Your look follows you

Dice and plaque customizations are tied to your Roll20 account, not a single game, so your saved look comes with you into every new campaign automatically.

8

Shared or per-player overlays

Each player gets a filtered overlay URL — perfect for individual scenes, separate cameras, or custom OBS layouts.

9

Server-side rules

Crit and fumble logic adapts to your game system — D&D 5e by default — with no need to reinstall anything.

10

Graceful fallbacks

The dice engine, fonts, sounds, and confetti are bundled locally. If anything fails, you still get a clean plaque with the total.

11

Self-updating capture script

The userscript keeps itself current, and a compatibility gate stops an outdated script cleanly instead of sending broken data — no manual reinstalls.

12

Preview without OBS

Open any overlay URL in your browser and press T to cycle through test rolls: normal → critical → fumble.

Step 2 of 5

Allow user scripts

Chrome 138+ and Edge need this one permission, or Banana Roll's panel never loads.

  1. Right-click the Tampermonkey icon and choose Manage extension.
  2. Turn on Allow user scripts.
  3. Reload your Roll20 tab.

Panel not appearing?
  • Tampermonkey is installed
  • The Banana Roll script is installed
  • Chrome/Edge: Allow User Scripts is enabled
  • Roll20 was reloaded after installing
  • The roll is public, not a private or GM roll

If the panel still does not appear, fully reload Roll20, confirm Tampermonkey is enabled on that site, and recheck the Chrome/Edge permission. If a test roll is private, the overlay will stay quiet on purpose.
